# Research disposition

Verdict: **accept** for the literature comparison and descriptive arithmetic.
See [verification](verification.md) and [checks](checks.json). This does not
activate parameters, establish field calibration or approve a model change.

The main quantities use **aboveground dry shoot biomass**. Savanna observations
provide actual complete-day water losses and a fixed harvest dry-mass
denominator, with incomplete/ambiguous observations retained and qualified.
Table-derived crop values use same-unit pot totals and same-harvest dry mass.
Numerical conversions and all 759 eligible daily sums are independently checked.

The new evidence supports rates in the tens of litres/kg dry shoot/day for
some young, actively transpiring plants, with much lower rates under drying
soils. It does not establish a universal grass/shrub factor, adult Jornada
coefficients or equivalence of water-support and biomass-production efficiencies.
Timing, environmental demand, leaf fraction and biomass definitions remain
material. No source/runtime/default/lock changes are accepted by this review.
