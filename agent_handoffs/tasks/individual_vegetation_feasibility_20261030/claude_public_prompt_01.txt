# Public literature and algorithm review

Review only public scientific literature and public source code. No private repository context is provided. Do not access local files, execute shell/code, use personal accounts, obtain paywalled credentials, or run models. Only public WebFetch/WebSearch tools are available. Prefer primary papers, authors' repositories, official code/docs; label anything not verified. Keep quotations to <=25 words per source and cite direct supporting URLs. Do not treat a citation title as evidence of an uninspected method.

Produce an independent, bounded research report (about 1500-2500 words) on precedents for spatial individual-plant demography/biomass/roots coupled to multilayer soil water, and generic CPU/GPU consequences. This is public model assessment, not implementation.

Investigate:
- Tietjen, Zehe & Jeltsch 2009 two-layer soil water (doi:10.1029/2007WR006589) and Tietjen et al. 2010 coupled grass/shrub cover (doi:10.1002/eco.70).
- STEPWAT2 (DrylandEcology/STEPWAT2 on GitHub), Palmquist et al. 2018 doi:10.1002/ecs2.2394 and SOILWAT2 (DrylandEcology/SOILWAT2). If inspecting code, distinguish current source from the public revision 003cb9b4adbf13d64a39d6d62fd95280adac27fc and SOILWAT2 submodule b05f410a9e96a5f26857bd37247fe7152e9861d8. Check spatial individual locations, root sharing, time scales, water-state persistence, global state and allocation choices; do not assume 'individual-based' means explicit X/Y or canopy overlap.
- Public ECOTONE ecological methods in Peters 2002, Ecological Modelling 152:5-32, doi:10.1016/S0304-3800(01)00460-4. Do not investigate any wind model or make claims about nonpublic local source/build status.
- Caylor et al. 2004 GCB 10:374-382 'Relationship between small-scale structural variability and simulated vegetation productivity across a regional moisture gradient in southern Africa'; Caylor & Shugart 2004 Landscape Ecology 19:401-415 'Simulated productivity of heterogeneous patches in southern African savanna landscapes using a canopy productivity model'; Caylor, Shugart & Rodriguez-Iturbe 2005 Ecosystems 8:17-32 'Tree canopy effects on simulated water stress in southern African savannas'; Caylor, D'Odorico & Rodriguez-Iturbe 2006 WRR 42:W07424 'On the ecohydrology of structurally heterogeneous semiarid landscapes'. Establish which are fixed spatial structure/productivity/water-stress models versus demographic models with recruitment/growth/death and whether they are one continuous implementation.
- Select at most 2-3 stronger additional public precedents for explicit individual positions, roots crossing resource cells, plant allometry and conservative water competition. Explain why they are closer than a generic vegetation model. Possible search leads are spatial individual vegetation/ecohydrology and tree/grass root competition; do not assume a particular model is suitable.

For each give concrete methods, scale, exactly what code/methods are reusable, adaptations and missing mechanisms. Include WUE/carbon versus biomass accounting and PFT/species distinctions where supported; plant recruitment/seeds/vegetative spread and terrain burial/erosion are questions, not claims every model implements them.

Separately review generic array/GPU design: structure-of-arrays versus linked records, sparse plant-cell-layer incidence and competing water uptake, race-free allocation/reductions, irregular spatial mapping and graph construction, persistent state versus phase-reusable scratch, float/index widths, stable IDs/RNG/restart, and CPU ecology between GPU numerical phases. Use symbolic sizes (N cells, L layers, P plants, E nonzero overlap entries) and clear bytes-per-entry assumptions; no invented measured speedups or hardware rankings, no cohorts as an equivalent replacement for individuals. Cite primary algorithm/docs sources where necessary.

Return a JSON object with keys report_markdown, primary_sources (list of URL + claim), unverified_or_uncertain, and questions_for_independent_review. Report evidence and inference distinctly. Do not choose a private project's model or architecture and do not claim any benchmarks or code runs.
